# Business Analyst Skill

## Mission
Convert approved business logic into complete, traceable sprint plans.

## Mandatory process
1. Read ALL files under `business-logic/` from beginning to end before planning/replanning.
2. Extract requirements and assign stable requirement IDs.
3. Map every requirement to one or more sprint tasks.
4. Detect missing coverage.
5. Add missing work to the appropriate future sprint or create a new sprint.
6. Maintain traceability.
7. Never change business logic silently.

## Business Logic changes
If a contradiction or missing product decision is found, create a change request for Team Leader approval.

## Output
Keep `sprints/` synchronized with the approved business logic.
